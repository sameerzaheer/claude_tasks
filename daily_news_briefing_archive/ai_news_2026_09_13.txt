Daily AI News Briefing — September 13, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Stealth security startup Accomplish disclosed leaky sandbox vulnerabilities it found across Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and Cursor (reported September 2026) — Cursor and OpenAI patched within about a week, while Anthropic took roughly 50 days and 30 releases to fix its "Beltdown" bug that let an untrusted repo escape the Claude Code sandbox without a permission prompt. (https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/accomplish-claims-leaky-sandboxes-in-claude-codex-cursor)

Sakana AI launched Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2.0 (announced September 11, 2026), a "multi-agent system delivered as one model" with a 1-million-token context window that dynamically routes each request across a pool of open-weight and specialized models, landing in the top two on seven of eight benchmarks without including a single frontier model in its pool. (https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/sakana-ai-launches-fugu-max-and-fugu-ultra-v2-for-cheaper-stronger-multi-agent-orchestration/)

🚀 Startups

(https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/09/3358622/0/en/implicity-secures-40m-growth-equity-funding-to-scale-its-ai-driven-cardiac-monitoring-platform-globally.html)

Optical-interconnect startup Ayar Labs raised an additional $150 million Series E (announced September 10, 2026), pushing its 2026 primary funding to $650 million and its valuation above $5 billion, to scale manufacturing of co-packaged optics that move data between AI chips faster than copper as clusters keep growing. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/10/ayar-labs-bags-150m-in-additional-series-e-funding-to-help-make-bigger-ai-chip-clusters/)

(https://northamericanmining.com/index.php/2026/09/11/iwt-project-among-four-selected-for-funding-by-doe/)

Zambia's Konkola Copper Mines partnered with Zensar Technologies (announced September 4, 2026) to deploy AI-enabled operations, intelligent automation and digital-workplace tools across its business, marking Zensar's first operational presence in Zambia. (https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/konkola-copper-mines-partners-with-zensar-to-accelerate-technology-led-business-transformation-302869911.html)
