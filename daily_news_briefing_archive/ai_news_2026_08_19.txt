🚀 Startups

(https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/groq-raises-350m-to-fuel-its-pivot-from-ai-chips-to-neocloud/)

AI inference-chip startup Etched raised $700 million at a $21 billion valuation led by Jane Street (announced August 18, 2026), roughly doubling its valuation in under a month as it challenges Nvidia with chips built into TSMC silicon. (https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/etcheds-valuation-doubles-to-21b-in-a-month/)
