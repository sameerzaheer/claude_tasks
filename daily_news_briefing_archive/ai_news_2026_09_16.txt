Daily AI News Briefing — September 16, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

DeepSeek officially released V4.1-Flash (September 10, 2026), its first natively multimodal Flash-tier model — a 552-billion-parameter MoE with a new causal encoder-decoder architecture (8B active params on input, 16B on output), a 1M-token context window and MIT-licensed weights, adding image understanding to the Flash line at as little as $0.22 per million input tokens. (https://llm-stats.com/models/deepseek-v4.1-flash)

Shanghai AI Laboratory quietly shipped Atria Dawn Preview (appeared on GitHub September 11, 2026, with a 140-author technical report following three days later), a 744-billion-parameter open-weight agentic MoE built on GLM-5.2 with a 256K context window, aimed at long-horizon research and engineering tasks that require continuous tool use and failure recovery. (https://aiweekly.co/alerts/shanghai-ai-lab-ships-atria-dawn-preview-a-744b-agentic-moe)

(https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/aeo-startup-profound-hits-unicorn-valuation-raises-180m-series-d-7-months-after-last-round/)

(https://techstartups.com/2026/09/15/samsung-backs-dutch-ai-chip-startup-euclyd-in-231m-funding-round-to-challenge-nvidia/)

⛏️ AI in Mining

At Electra Mining Africa 2026 in Johannesburg (September 7-11, 2026), exhibitors including Kal Tire showcased AI-enabled tyre-monitoring technology — such as electromagnetic non-destructive scanning that spots cracks and defects early — that is cutting mining tyre downtime by up to 20% while improving safety and sustainability. (https://www.miningweekly.com/article/ai-helping-reduce-mining-tyre-downtime-by-up-to-20-electra-mining-africa-showcases-2026-09-09)

Epiroc expanded its Deep Automation platform for autonomous underground mine trucks to operate in surface environments too (announced September 3, 2026), using 3D LiDAR for localization, mapping and obstacle detection to enable seamless underground-to-surface autonomous hauling and dumping cycles. (https://rockproducts.com/2026/09/03/epirocs-expands-capabilities-of-autonomous-underground-mine-trucks/)

🌐 Browser Automation Startups
