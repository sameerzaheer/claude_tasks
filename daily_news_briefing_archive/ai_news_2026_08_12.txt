Daily AI News Briefing — August 12, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Lightning (August 11, 2026), a 30-billion-parameter open-source mixture-of-experts model (3B active params) distilled from Nemotron 3 Ultra that runs on a single consumer GPU and ships alongside NeMo Switchyard, a router that sends each agent request to the most efficient model. (https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-lightning-switchyard-rtx-dgx/)

ByteDance opened the public developer API for Seedance 2.5 (August 7, 2026), its video-generation model that produces up to 30 seconds of synchronized audio-video in a single pass with timestamp-level editing and expanded multimodal references. (https://evolink.ai/blog/seedance-2-5-api-status)

(https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260714397606/en/TerraFirma-Raises-$115M-to-Accelerate-Construction-on-Earth-and-Beyond)

📋 AI in Procurement & Proposal Writing

GovCon ProposalEngine's weekly RFP watch (August 10, 2026) found federal agencies pressing ahead with major solicitations — including Space Force's $300 million ground-ops contract and DHS's $1.5 billion counter-drone vehicle program — even as the suspended CMMC Phase 2 cybersecurity certification requirements undergo a reform review that's complicating compliance planning for proposal teams. (https://govconproposalengine.com/blog/govcon-rfp-watch-2026-08-10/)

(https://federalnewsnetwork.com/cybersecurity/2026/07/pentagon-suspends-cmmc-phase-two-requirements-launches-review-of-program/)
