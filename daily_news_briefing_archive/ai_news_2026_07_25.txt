Daily AI News Briefing — July 25, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5 (July 24, 2026), a cheaper alternative to Fable 5 with a new low/medium/high effort toggle to balance cost and capability, becoming the default model on Claude Max and the strongest available on Claude Pro. (https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)

Google published its first AI & Economy ATLAS report (July 23, 2026), analyzing 15 million Gemini interactions across 800 occupations and finding AI use has reached 68% of occupations but fully automates fewer than 10% of tasks. (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/research/understanding-the-ai-economy/)

🏗️ AI in AEC, Construction & Engineering

Autodesk released a Tech Preview of its official open-source Revit Public MCP Server (announced June 17, 2026), giving any MCP-compatible AI agent — Claude, Cursor, Copilot — 102 tools to read and write live BIM data directly. (https://www.autodesk.com/blogs/aec/2026/06/17/revit-public-mcp-server/)

Three ex-SpaceX engineers launched 1872, an AI-powered autonomous steel fabrication factory in Cincinnati, closing a $15 million seed round (announced July 22, 2026) to automate heavy steel fabrication for modular construction and infrastructure. (https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jul/22/1872-launches-autonomous-steel-fabrication-factory/)


📋 AI in Procurement & Proposal Writing

The Technology Modernization Fund is soliciting federal agency proposals for AI adoption and permitting-modernization projects, with initial submissions due July 24, 2026 and selections expected by September 30. (https://www.executivegov.com/articles/tmf-proposal-calls-federal-ai-permitting)

Contract-AI platform IntelAgree launched Saige Assist: Agent (announced July 15, 2026), a general-purpose contract agent in private beta that works from a company's own clause library, playbooks and negotiation history to answer questions, compare versions, redline, and make approval-gated updates.
