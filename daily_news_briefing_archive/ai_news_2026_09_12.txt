Daily AI News Briefing — September 12, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Meta launched Muse (announced September 8, 2026), a personal AI agent that books travel, fills forms and checks out on a user's behalf from a visible in-browser session running inside a dedicated secure cloud VM, offered free with $20 and $100 monthly tiers. (https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)

Anthropic accused Chinese labs Moonshot AI and DeepSeek of covertly routing millions of customer requests to Claude models and passing the outputs off as their own — including nearly 300,000 Moonshot requests in one 10-day span and 12+ million DeepSeek "distillation" attempts over 14 days in July — to train their competing models (reported September 11, 2026). (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/chinese-ai-labs-moonshot-deepseek-alibaba-anthropic.html)

(https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/10/3359208/0/en/nokia-launches-ai-and-edge-platform-to-help-mining-and-construction-industries-public-safety-and-defense-organizations-run-safer-more-resilient-field-operations.html)

Mariana Minerals raised a $310 million Series B at a $1.5 billion valuation (announced August 2026) led by Khosla Ventures, with a16z and Breakthrough Energy returning, to build and operate US copper and lithium mines using its own AI, sensor and robotics stack rather than selling software to other miners.
