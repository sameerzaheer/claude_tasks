OpenAI disclosed that its unreleased Astra model is the first to cross the company's "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold (announced September 1, 2026), scoring 100% on ExploitBench and independently chaining two zero-day exploits, prompting OpenAI to restrict Astra's most powerful cyber capabilities to a vetted coalition of partner organizations.
