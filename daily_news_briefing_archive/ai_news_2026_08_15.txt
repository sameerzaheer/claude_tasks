Daily AI News Briefing — August 15, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Stanford and Arc Institute researchers used AI genome models (Evo 1/Evo 2) to design complete viral genomes from scratch, building 16 that became fully functional bacteriophages able to infect and kill E. coli — some outperforming natural viruses — in a Science study (published August 6, 2026) that raises both medical and biosecurity questions. (https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/07/ai-designed-viruses-infect-bacteria-science-study/)

Anthropic began embedding invisible watermarks and signed C2PA provenance metadata in all Claude-generated text and files worldwide, without an opt-out, starting August 2, 2026 — a global rollout driven by the EU AI Act's transparency mandate. (https://www.forbes.com/sites/anishasircar/2026/08/13/claude-will-now-leave-a-watermark-on-everything-it-writes-what-does-that-mean/)

(https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260810580127/en/LT-Technology-Services-Launches-AgenticIQ-an-End-to-End-Agentic-AI-Platform-for-Engineering-Manufacturing-and-Customer-Experience)

(https://www.einpresswire.com/article/930559604/formx-raises-12-5-million-to-build-the-platform-transforming-residential-construction)

📋 AI in Procurement & Proposal Writing

New Tussell tracker data (reported August 14, 2026) found the UK public sector has awarded 2,129 AI contracts worth £5 billion since 2018, with 2026 already the record year by value, though AI still accounts for only about 4% of total government IT spend — Palantir and Microsoft lead the supplier rankings. (https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-08-14-public-sector-ai-contracts-5bn/)

New B2G sales startup RFPs.ai is building a multi-agent AI platform (reported August 10, 2026) that continuously discovers, ingests and ranks government RFPs against a company's capabilities and certifications, with additional agents in development to flag opportunities from council meetings and budgets before an RFP is even published. (https://aithority.com/machine-learning/rfps-ai-builds-ai-platform-for-government-procurement-discovery/)
