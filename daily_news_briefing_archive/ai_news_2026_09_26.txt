(https://the-decoder.com/alibaba-launches-qwen-audio-3-1-with-five-new-models-and-slashes-ai-audio-prices-by-up-to-95-percent/)

(https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/ando-eyes-slack-as-it-builds-team-messaging-platform-for-humans-and-agents-to-work-together/)

(https://news.crunchbase.com/ai/verifying-ai-agents-baselayer-35m-raise/)

(https://im-mining.com/2026/09/23/barrick-to-put-avathon-ai-solution-to-work-at-north-american-assets/)

DGC Africa's Vision AI platform (reported August 28, 2026) is giving mining operators real-time visibility into the condition of assets in hazardous or hard-to-reach areas, supporting predictive maintenance and reducing unplanned equipment outages. (https://www.miningweekly.com/article/vision-ai-enhances-asset-visibility-across-mining-operations-2026-08-28)

🌐 Browser Automation Startups

Insurance AI startup Strada launched browser automation (announced September 24, 2026) that lets its agents record and replay workflows inside carrier portals and legacy systems that have no API, with full run-time logging for audit and no engineering work required to set up. (https://itbusinessnet.com/2026/09/strada-launches-browser-automation-for-carrier-portals-and-legacy-systems-without-apis/)

A coalition of banks including Bank of America, NatWest and Capital One warned regulators (September 22, 2026) that AI browser-based shopping agents are advancing faster than fraud protections and consumer safeguards, urging mandatory disclosure whenever an AI agent handles a transaction. (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/banks-warn-ai-shopping-bots-raise-scam-fraud-data-privacy-risks.html)
