🤖 AI & LLMs

Novo Nordisk and Anthropic announced a collaboration (September 16, 2026) under which the drugmaker will use Anthropic's frontier models and test its new Claude Science workbench across specific R&D workflows to speed biological reasoning and accelerate the path from research insight to new medicines. (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/16/3363106/0/en/novo-and-anthropic-will-collaborate-to-advance-drug-discovery-with-claude.html)

🚀 Startups

(https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/caddi-raises-114m-at-1-2b-valuation-to-bring-manufacturing-ai-to-north-america/)

Rune raised a $40 million Series A led by Spark Capital (announced September 16, 2026) and debuted RELIC, a modular data-center system that installs directly at solar sites to turn curtailed, otherwise-wasted solar power into AI compute capacity within as little as six weeks. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/rune-raises-40m-for-its-modular-solar-powered-data-centers/)
