Daily AI News Briefing — September 29, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Anthropic launched Claude Sonnet 5.5 (September 28, 2026), the second model in the Claude 5.5 family after Opus 5.5, generating output more than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and cutting cost per task by up to 30% at unchanged API prices ($2/$10 per million tokens), and it is the first Sonnet to ship with cyber safeguards like those on Anthropic's more capable models. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/28/anthropic-debuts-claude-sonnet-5-5-running-30-faster-than-the-previous-generation-ai-model/)

Xiaomi open-sourced its MiMo-V2.6 series under an MIT license (announced September 22, 2026), including the omnimodal MiMo-V2.6-Pro and Flash models with 1-million-token context windows plus more than 7,000 reinforcement-learning environments, with the Pro model topping the open-weights leaderboards. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/xiaomi-introduces-mimo-v2-6-series-open-source-ai-model-family/)

🚀 Startups

Legal-tech provider Mitratech acquired AI-native agent-orchestration startup BotDojo (announced September 28, 2026; terms undisclosed) to power its ARIES AI and move from a legal "system of record" to a "System of Action" that can execute tasks autonomously, with two-way MCP support arriving this autumn. (https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2026/09/28/mitratech-buys-botdojo-for-agent-orchestration/)

AI coding startup Cognition (maker of Devin) raised $2 billion in a Series E led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel at a $48 billion valuation (reported September 8, 2026), nearly doubling its May valuation of $26 billion as run-rate revenue grew from $492 million to nearly $900 million. (https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/cognition-hits-48b-valuation-signaling-investors-believe-ai-coding-is-far-from-a-winner-take-all-market/)

⛏️ AI in Mining

Sandvik is presenting its AutoMine® Aura automation platform to the global mining industry at its Future of Mining 2026 event in Tampere, Finland (September 1-3, 2026), pitching it as a first-of-its-kind system for connecting AI, robotics and autonomous equipment across the mine. (https://im-mining.com/2026/09/01/sandvik-brings-global-mining-leaders-together-at-future-of-mining-2026/)

SAP Canada published a look at how AI is forging the autonomous future of Canadian mining (September 2026), arguing miners are moving beyond the "digital mine" toward AI-driven planning and operations. (https://news.sap.com/canada/2026/09/beyond-the-digital-mine-how-ai-is-forging-the-autonomous-future-of-canadian-mining/)

🌐 Browser Automation Startups

Perplexity's Comet agentic browser launched on Android (August 19, 2026), billed as the first agentic AI browser built for mobile, and its agentic browsing has since been integrated into Samsung Internet. (https://www.itechguides.com/openai-and-perplexity-enter-browser-wars-to-take-on-chrome-what-changed-by-august-2026/)

The browser-agent tooling landscape is consolidating into consumer AI browsers (Comet, Dia, Opera Neon), open-source frameworks (Browser Use, Stagehand, Skyvern) and managed infrastructure (Browserbase, Steel, Firecrawl), with Browser Use ($17M raised) and Anchor Browser ($6M seed) among the notable startups. (https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/best-browser-agents)
