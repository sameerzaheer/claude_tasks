## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- A PwC and Minerals Council South Africa study, covered Nov 23, finds that AI for predictive maintenance (less unplanned downtime) and real-time monitoring of mine conditions (better safety) is already showing results in South African mines. The same piece looks at who owns the IP when miners and AI vendors work together. ([Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/who-owns-the-ip-in-ai-led-mining-collaborations-184676a))

## 🔧 New Hardware

- Imperial College London's VecTile is a 6 mm-thin, flexible 4×4 tactile array that senses force in three directions (3-DoF). Labs can build it from cheap outsourced flexible PCBs and 3D-printed silicone molds. One secondary source puts the sensor cost at about $3.57, but the paper excerpts I found do not confirm it. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31472)) *Opportunity: touch sensing for robot fingertips and grippers costs a few dollars instead of hundreds, which suits grippers for picking fragile produce and e-commerce items, collecting teleoperation and glove data for robot training, and smart prosthetics.*
