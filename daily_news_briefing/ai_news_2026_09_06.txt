Daily AI News Briefing — September 6, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Anthropic said Claude autonomously produced the first end-to-end, computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in the Lean language over 11 days (announced September 4, 2026), generating 13 million lines of Lean code and proving 30,300 theorems along the way. (https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)

OpenAI pledged $1 billion for "Daybreak for Frontline Defenders" (announced September 3, 2026), subsidizing access to its Daybreak cyber-defense models for water utilities, power grids, local governments and nonprofits, alongside a new "Defense Factory" architecture for continuously finding and patching vulnerabilities. (https://openai.com/index/daybreak-for-frontline-defenders/)
