# Daily News Briefing — 2026-11-17

## 🤖 AI / LLMs / AI Products

- AMD closed its roughly $8.2B all-stock purchase of Fei-Fei Li's World Labs on Nov 16. World Labs builds AI models that generate interactive 3D worlds, and Li joins AMD as EVP and Chief Scientist, a move that gives AMD a frontier "spatial intelligence" research lab of its own. ([GlobeNewswire / AMD](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/16/3403615/0/en/amd-completes-acquisition-of-world-labs.html))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Vivox AI and beSirius announced a partnership (Nov 9) that combines their AI tools to automate the paperwork-heavy ESG, permitting and regulatory compliance work mining companies do. ([Canadian Mining Journal](https://www.canadianminingjournal.com/news/vivox-ai-besirius-partner-to-streamline-mining-compliance/))

## 🔍 AI in Mineral Exploration

- Fitzroy Minerals has started a 5,000 m drill program in Chile on nine untested copper targets that came from a machine-learning study with Fleet Space Technologies. The model excluded ground that was already mined out or within 200 m of existing holes. This was announced Oct 23, and there was no fresher AI-exploration news this week. ([Streetwise Reports](https://www.streetwisereports.com/article/2026/10/23/machine-learning-identifies-9-untested-copper-targets-in-chile-as-5-000-meter-drilling-program-begins.html))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- At ROBOTWORLD 2026 (Nov 4–7), oToBrite showed a compact 3D stereo vision camera module rated IP69K, plus a GMSL2 link to NVIDIA Holoscan with YUAN. *Opportunity: a depth camera that survives high-pressure, high-temperature washdown means robots can work where cleaning or dirt has kept them out. Lucrative uses include meat and poultry processing and packing lines, agricultural harvesters and dairy robots, car-wash and fleet-cleaning automation, and perception for dusty, wet mining equipment.* ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/otobrite-showcases-3d-stereo-vision-camera-and-teams-up-with-yuan-for-gmsl2-to-hsb-demo-at-robotworld-2026-302921593.html))
