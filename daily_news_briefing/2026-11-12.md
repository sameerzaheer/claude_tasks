# Daily News Briefing — November 12, 2026

## 🤖 AI & LLMs

- Meta Superintelligence Lab released Muse Ember (Nov 9), a small proprietary model that runs the routine steps inside AI agents while a bigger model plans; it has a 1M-token context window, costs $0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens (the same as Claude Haiku 5.5), and Artificial Analysis ranks it second in its price class. ([The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/meta-muse-ember-launch/))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Nokia and SITECH Brasil formed a partnership to bring "physical AI" to Brazilian mines (Nov 5), starting with AI fatigue detection for heavy-equipment operators on Nokia's Cognitive Operations platform, with plans to expand across Latin America. ([International Mining](https://im-mining.com/2026/11/05/sitech-and-nokia-bringing-physical-ai-to-mining-operations-in-brazil/))

## 🔎 AI in Mineral Exploration

- KoBold Metals' CTO Tom Hunt called Chile "definitely under-explored" for copper and said KoBold's AI discovery tools could work there, but the company has no Chilean project yet because the best ground is already held and it hasn't found a partner. He offered no drilling or survey data to back the claim. ([The Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/chile-kobold-metals-underexplored-copper-2026/))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

- Munich-based Agile Robots raised $500M in a round led by SoftBank, with Nvidia, Hitachi and T.Capital joining, at an $8.5B valuation to push into humanoids; its total funding is now about $2B (Nov 12). ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/agile-robots-softbank-500m-humanoid))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- StarBot opened reservations for Spark, a 3-ft, 35-lb humanoid with 22 degrees of freedom that it says is made entirely in the US, at $7,999 ($5,999 for the first 100 orders); no shipping date has been published. *Opportunity: a US-made humanoid under $8k is not affected by the FCC's ban on foreign-made robots, so schools, universities and defense-adjacent labs could buy it; lucrative uses include STEM curriculum kits, training-data collection services for robot-learning startups, and a fleet for testing robot software before it goes on full-size robots.* ([Humanoid.guide](https://humanoid.guide/?p=21369))
