## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Zurich's Enact Intelligence (covered October 22 for its $30M pre-seed) measures force inside its robots' own motors instead of using separate force sensors, which it calls too costly and fragile for factories. **Opportunity:** this favors suppliers of actuators and drives with built-in torque sensing and precise current/force control. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/enact-intelligence-30m-pre-seed-robot-touch))
- Xense Robotics showed a multimodal tactile sensor line at ICRA 2026, plus a wearable two-finger data-collection device pairing a visuo-tactile sensor with an IMU and encoder. **Opportunity:** touch-rich demonstration data is scarce, so low-cost tactile capture rigs for training robot policies have a ready buyer base. ([Humanoid Robotics Technology](https://humanoidroboticstechnology.com/event-news/xense-robotics-tactile-intelligence-icra-2026/))
