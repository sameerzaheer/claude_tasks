Daily AI News Briefing — July 31, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Google DeepMind unveiled Gemini Robotics 2 (July 30, 2026), a three-model suite — including an on-device variant and the ER 2 embodied-reasoning model — that gives humanoid robots whole-body coordination and hit a 92% success rate on complex multi-step dexterity tasks. (https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/)

The European Commission formally opened its call for AI "gigafactory" proposals (July 30, 2026), offering up to €10 billion in public funding toward a €30 billion push to build up to seven AI compute hubs and close Europe's chip gap with the US and China. (https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_26_1708)

🚀 Startups

Devin-maker Cognition acquired Poke, the personality-driven AI assistant native to iMessage and Telegram, in a low-nine-figure deal (announced July 23, 2026), aiming to bring Poke's conversational style into its coding agent. (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/)

Fei-Fei Li's World Labs acquired robotics simulation startup SceniX (announced July 21, 2026), marking its biggest step into embodied AI by pairing its 3D world-generation models with SceniX's sim-to-real robot training tech. (https://techstartups.com/2026/07/21/world-labs-acquires-scenix-to-bridge-generative-world-models-and-physical-robotics-for-embodied-ai/)

🏗️ AI in AEC, Construction & Engineering

Austin-based TerraFirma, founded by former SpaceX engineers, raised $115 million including a $100 million Series A led by Kleiner Perkins (announced July 14, 2026) to scale its AI-enabled, semi-autonomous heavy construction equipment fleet. (https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260714397606/en/TerraFirma-Raises-$115M-to-Accelerate-Construction-on-Earth-and-Beyond)

Amsterdam-based Monumental, founded by ex-Palantir engineers, raised a $32 million Series B led by Khosla Ventures (announced July 15, 2026) to expand its fleet of 150 autonomous bricklaying robots from Europe into the US. (https://fortune.com/2026/07/15/construction-robotics-startup-monumental-raises-32-million-from-khosla-ventures-to-tackle-labor-shortages/)

Rowe Price and Unity joining as customers. (https://itbusinessnet.com/2026/07/omnea-triples-revenue-in-twelve-months-as-enterprises-choose-agentic-operating-system-for-procurement/)

(https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/07/28/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/kt-automation-introduces-ai-platform-that-understands-industrial-safety-security-automation-procurement/2393048)
