## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Sonair unveiled what it calls the first safety-certified 3D ultrasonic sensor for human-robot collaboration. **Opportunity:** low-cost certified safety sensing lets integrators put mobile robots and cobots next to people without expensive lidar-based safety systems. ([Robotics 24/7](https://www.robotics247.com/topic/category/lidar))
- China's lidar leaders Hesai and RoboSense are pivoting hard into robotics, with RoboSense reporting a huge jump in robotics lidar shipments in Q1 2026 (from a low base). **Opportunity:** volume lidar for robots is driving costs down, so small makers can build affordable robot-specific perception modules on top of it. ([SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3347995/chinas-lidar-giants-pivot-robotics-jensen-huang-praises-chinese-supply-chain))
