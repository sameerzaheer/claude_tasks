## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- Benewake's SR18 is a palm-sized 360° 3D lidar for mobile robots: 18 channels, 15 cm–30 m range, 190 g and 5 W. It is in mass production and goes on sale for **$299** through Mouser and Amazon from Nov 30. *Opportunity: 360° 3D perception now costs about as much as a hobby part, so outdoor and small robots can use real 3D SLAM and obstacle avoidance. Lucrative uses include wire-free robotic lawn mowers for commercial landscaping, compact cleaning robots for floors and solar farms, low-cost security and inspection quadrupeds, and retrofit kits that let forklifts and carts navigate on their own.* ([Macau Business / PR Newswire](https://macaubusiness.com/benewake-introduces-compact-power-efficient-sr-series-360-3d-lidars-for-mobile-robots-sr18-enters-mass-production/))
