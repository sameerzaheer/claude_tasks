Perplexity rolled out "Hybrid Compute" for its Mac app (announced September 1, 2026), letting users run sensitive AI tasks locally via a one-click download of a Perplexity-tuned Qwen 3.8 27B model while the rest of the task runs in the cloud. (https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/01/perplexity-launches-privacy-minded-hybrid-compute-ai-feature-for-mac/)

(https://venturebeat.com/technology/meta-says-muse-spark-1-3-has-frontier-performance-but-its-best-results-come-from-a-model-developers-cant-broadly-use-yet)
