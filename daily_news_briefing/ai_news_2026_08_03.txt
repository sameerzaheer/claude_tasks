Daily AI News Briefing — August 3, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

OpenAI announced on August 1, 2026 that an internal version of its next model, Astra, produced ten new machine-checked proofs for math and theoretical computer science problems that had been open for at least a decade — including the first explicit construction of a non-sofic group — publishing a 249-page manuscript with Lean 4 certificates on GitHub for roughly $2,000 in total compute. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/02/openais-astra-solves-10-long-open-math-problems-publishes-proofs/)

The EU AI Act's general application began on August 2, 2026, handing the AI Office and member-state authorities enforcement power over GPAI models and activating new transparency rules requiring chatbots to disclose they're AI and AI-generated content to carry machine-readable marks. (https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)

🏗️ AI in AEC, Construction & Engineering

Compliance startup Dili raised $21.7 million (a $15M Series A led by Khosla Ventures plus its earlier $6.7M seed, announced July 30, 2026) to expand AI that converts unstructured project records into structured data and checks them against federal wage, safety and environmental rules across 700+ infrastructure and construction projects. (https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/dili-raises-15-million-to-bring-ai-compliance-to-the-infrastructure-boom/)

SoftBank is in talks to acquire Swiss autonomous-construction-equipment startup Gravis Robotics for more than $500 million (reported late July 2026), folding the ETH Zurich spinout into its new Roze physical-AI holding company as part of a broader push into AI-driven heavy machinery. (https://techfundingnews.com/softbank-eyes-500m-gravis-robotics-deal-after-completing-its-boston-dynamics-exit/)

(https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-harness-report-reveals-enterprise-ai-spend-has-outgrown-the-systems-built-to-track-it-302837776.html)

(https://cporising.com/2026/07/27/ai-rising-2026-pt-1-state-of-procurement-series-transition-to-ai-first-procurement/)
