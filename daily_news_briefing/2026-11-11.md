# Daily News Briefing — November 11, 2026

## 🤖 AI & LLMs

- NVIDIA unveiled GR00T 2 at CoRL on Nov 9, an open 2B-parameter "world action model" that jointly predicts video and robot actions, trained partly on 100,000 hours of first-person human video it is also releasing, with weights due in December. ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/nvidia-gr00t-2-robots-human-video-corl))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Impossible Metals says its AI-guided autonomous robot hovered above the Pacific seabed at more than 4,000 m and selectively picked about 450 g of polymetallic nodules without a riser pipe; a larger version still needs ISA or US approval (announced Nov 9). ([Baird Maritime](https://www.bairdmaritime.com/offshore/undersea-mining/ai-guided-deep-sea-mining-robot-tested-in-the-pacific-ocean))

## 🔎 AI in Mineral Exploration

- Fleet Space launched its Tau 3.8 model (Nov 10); at ATEX's Valeriano copper-gold project in Chile, it predicted copper grades on held-out drillholes with 38% less error than conventional kriging (vendor claim). ([Business Intelligence](https://businessintelligence.mo/2026/11/11/ai-trained-on-ten-million-synthetic-earths-outperforms-conventional-copper-estimates-at-tsxv-listed-atexs-valeriano-project/))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

- Path Robotics, which automates industrial welding, raised a $300M Series E led by Georgian at a $2.45B post-money valuation, about four times its previous valuation (Nov 10). ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/10/industrial-automation-startup-path-robotics-raises-300m-in-funding/))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- Amazon's Ring unveiled the Ring Rover Cam, a $999.99 security patrol robot for stores and offices that ships Dec 16, plus a $249.99 indoor Drone Cam that is awaiting FCC approval (Nov 10). *Opportunity: a sub-$1k mobile camera robot brings patrol automation to small businesses, so there is room for add-on services such as after-hours retail inventory scans, warehouse leak and temperature checks, and remote-monitoring subscriptions for property managers.* ([Basic Tutorials](https://basic-tutorials.com/news/ring-drone-after-six-years-drone-cam-and-rover-cam-launch-from-ring-robotics/))
