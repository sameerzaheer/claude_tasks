## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- At bauma CHINA (Nov 24–25), Hong Kong-listed CiDi launched Yura, an AI agent that coordinates drilling, blasting, loading, hauling and dumping across a whole mine, and signed deals to extend its autonomous systems in Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia and Anhui (vendor press release). ([Manila Times / PR Newswire](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/25/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/cidi-unveils-expanded-heavy-duty-embodied-ai-portfolio-at-bauma-china-2026-extending-automation-from-surface-mining-to-underground-operations/2477732))

## 🔎 AI in Mineral Exploration

- Canadian Mining Journal reports (Nov 24) that Stratum AI's deep-learning models of weathering and material type are now built into the published mineral resource estimate for St Barbara's Simberi gold project in Papua New Guinea, one of the first times an AI model has gone into a formal resource estimate instead of a side study. ([Canadian Mining Journal](https://canadianminingjournal.com/news/stratum-ai-model-feeds-simberis-mineral-resource-estimate))
