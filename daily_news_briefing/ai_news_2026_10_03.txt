UK-listed First Class Metals signed a non-binding letter of intent to join Rosor Exploration's AI-enabled "Project Field-to-Insight" (October 2026 to September 2027), using drone survey data over 54 km² at its Sunbeam Project in Ontario to speed up geophysical processing and interpretation. (https://investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/first-class-metals--fcm/first-class-metals-signs-ai-exploration-loi/9803057)

🌐 Browser Automation Startups

Shopify now lets AI agents running in a buyer's browser read, update and complete checkout on eligible merchant storefronts (after buyer authorization) via its Universal Commerce Protocol, with no merchant configuration needed. (https://aichief.com/news/shopify-opens-checkout-to-browser-based-ai-agents/)

Browserbase, the browser-infrastructure startup (about $67.5M raised), added agent runs as a counted entitlement on every plan in September 2026 (3 on Free, 15 on Developer, 50 on Startup), signaling a shift toward monetizing hosted agents. (https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/browserbase)
