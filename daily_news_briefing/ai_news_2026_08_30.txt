🚀 Startups

Physical-AI startup Transfyr launched out of stealth with a $25 million seed round led by General Catalyst (announced August 26, 2026), building sensor- and multimodal-AI observability tools to capture the lab activity, equipment telemetry and hands-on adjustments that never make it into scientific papers. (https://techstartups.com/2026/08/26/transfyr-launches-with-25m-in-funding-to-bring-physical-ai-to-scientific-labs/)

Minneapolis-based Yardstik raised a $30 million Series B led by Harbert Growth Partners (announced August 27, 2026) to expand its AI-driven post-hire workforce fraud and credential-monitoring platform, bringing total funding to $65 million on 149% year-over-year revenue growth. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/27/workplace-fraud-monitoring-startup-yardstik-raises-30m-to-help-employers-keep-tabs-on-their-staff/)

(https://www.defenseone.com/policy/2026/08/ai-army-contract-lawsuit/415261/)

RFP.co launched an AI-powered platform (announced August 26, 2026) that helps businesses discover, evaluate, track and respond to RFPs, RFQs and other procurement opportunities scattered across government portals and public databases, pairing centralized search with AI-assisted proposal drafting. (https://digitalmarketreports.com/news/90239/rfpco-launches-ai-powered-platform-to-help-businesses-find-and-win-more-rfp-opportunities/)
