## Hardware (robotics/sensors) automation opportunities
- TrendForce says a single dexterous humanoid hand may carry 80–200 pressure sensors plus IMU and ToF (Sept 10 bulletin). **Opportunity:** per-robot sensor counts this high make cheap, miniaturized tactile and ToF modules a volume business. ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP260910XF))
- The FCC launched an import review of foreign advanced robots (reported Aug 4), with US reliance on Asian components expected to push sourcing toward regional supply chains. **Opportunity:** domestic or allied makers of actuators, lidar and sensors gain an opening as buyers look for non-Asian suppliers. ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/research/robot))
