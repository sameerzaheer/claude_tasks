## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Allegro MicroSystems expanded its robotics portfolio at electronica 2026 with TMR and Hall-effect angle sensors pitched as replacements for resolvers and optical encoders in robot joints. *Opportunity: cheaper, more compact joint-position sensing lowers the bill of materials for new actuators and retrofit kits.* ([Embedded Computing Design](https://embeddedcomputing.com/application/industrial/automation-robotics/electronica-2026-allegro-expands-robotics-portfolio))
