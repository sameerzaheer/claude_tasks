🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

China's lidar leaders Hesai and RoboSense are pivoting into robotics (Hesai budgeting ~200M yuan for robot "eyes and muscles"; RoboSense tops GGII's Q1 2026 3D-lidar shipments in five robotics categories) — opportunity: cheap robot-grade lidar is going mainstream, so differentiated Western/allied supply (tariff-resilient, safety-certified) and sensor-fusion modules have room. (https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3347995/chinas-lidar-giants-pivot-robotics-jensen-huang-praises-chinese-supply-chain)

Tariffs on lidar used in warehouse, manufacturing and healthcare robots are pushing localized manufacturing and supplier diversification, alongside Ouster's Rev8 digital lidar (color sensing, ~2x range/resolution, targeting functional-safety certification) — opportunity: regional lidar/sensor assembly and cost-optimized designs for robotics OEMs. (https://korthosrobotics.com/research/q1-results-show-robotics-lidar-is-becoming-measurable)
