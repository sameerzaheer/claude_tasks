## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- EACON and Zijin's Tibet Julong report all 60 battery-electric haul trucks at the Zhibula copper mine now run autonomously above 5,400 m, with 18 more trucks being retrofitted at the adjacent Julong mine (Nov 4 press release; vendor-reported). ([PR Newswire via Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/04/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/60-autonomous-electric-haul-trucks-achieve-site-wide-operation-at-5400m-copper-mine/2461513))
- International Mining (Nov 2) argues high-altitude mines are going autonomous to limit personnel exposure, citing Zhibula and Caterpillar's autonomous haulage at about 3,500 m at Quellaveco, Peru. ([International Mining](https://im-mining.com/2026/11/02/eacon-on-why-high-altitude-mines-are-going-autonomous/))

## 🔧 Hardware/Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Agilink launched a tactile data glove after a 72-step robotic-hand factory trial (98% task success) and plans to offer its tactile data and manipulation models to robot makers (Nov 4). *Opportunity: demand for human-demonstration tactile data and dense fingertip sensors is growing, so low-cost tactile sensing and data-capture hardware is open ground.* ([KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/agilink-launches-tactile-data-glove-after-72-step-robotic-hand-trial))
- Korean startup Aidin Robotics has a 15 mm six-axis force-torque sensor (a 10 mm version is in development) and a second-generation robot hand due at year-end (Oct 28). *Opportunity: miniature force sensing is what lets robots take over delicate factory assembly jobs still done by people.* ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/technology/2026/10/28/korean-startups-force-sensors-aim-to-automate-factory-work))
