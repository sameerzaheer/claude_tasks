## 🔧 Hardware & Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Qualcomm acquired robot-motion-software firm PickNik Robotics, and NVIDIA released Isaac ROS 5.0 with AI-agent capabilities (September). **Opportunity:** as chipmakers and platforms standardize the compute and software stack, differentiation shifts to the sensors and actuators that plug into it. ([Robotics 24/7 September recap](https://www.robotics247.com/podcast/september-2026-robotics-recap))
- LG Innotek and TDK are co-developing a vision sensing module (optics plus IMU, position sensors and microphones) and a tactile module for physical-AI robots, targeting 2027 (July). **Opportunity:** large suppliers are still a year out, leaving room for startups to ship tactile and multi-sensor modules for robot builders now. ([Asia Economy](https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026072808161902349))
