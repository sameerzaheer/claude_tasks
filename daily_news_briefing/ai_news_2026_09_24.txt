(https://im-mining.com/2026/09/23/jevons-robotics-launches-battery-electric-artev1000-pre-split-system/)

(https://news.sap.com/canada/2026/09/beyond-the-digital-mine-how-ai-is-forging-the-autonomous-future-of-canadian-mining/)
