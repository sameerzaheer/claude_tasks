## 🔍 AI in Mineral Exploration

- MineTRACE is a web-based research system that helps geologists judge mineral prospectivity by reasoning over geological evidence and showing which data each conclusion rests on. It covers eight commodities: copper, gold, nickel, tungsten, tin, cobalt, tantalum and manganese. This is an arXiv preprint from September 2026, not a discovery. ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02060))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- At its STM32 Summit (Nov 3–4), STMicroelectronics unveiled the STM32A microcontroller family (A4, A5 and A9), a unified platform for safety-critical, real-time control in cars, industry and robots. The chips are sampling to customers now, with wider availability in 2027. *Opportunity: cheap, safety-certifiable chips that run deterministic control sit between a robot's AI computer and its motors, so robot makers can meet functional-safety rules without custom silicon. Lucrative uses include safety-rated joint controllers for humanoids and cobots, motion control for warehouse robots and autonomous forklifts, and drive-by-wire retrofit kits for off-road and mining vehicles.* ([Electronic Design](https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/55417773/electronic-design-stm32-summit-2026-introduces-stm32a-stm32g5-stm32h7p-and-ai-tools))
