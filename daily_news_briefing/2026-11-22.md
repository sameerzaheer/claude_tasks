## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Status update (Nov 19): Komatsu, its subsidiary EARTHBRAIN and TIER IV are still aiming to run autonomous HD785/HD605 rigid and HM400/460 articulated dump trucks at Japanese quarries and civil-engineering sites in fiscal 2027. The trucks are meant to run full load-haul-dump cycles, with TIER IV's Autoware software doing the driving. This summary is secondary and reports no new milestone. ([Geomechanics.io](https://www.geomechanics.io/news/article/komatsu-driverless-dump-truck-systems-deployment-and-control-insights-for-engineers))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

- Boston-based Strive Robotics, founded by MIT alumni, raised about $3M in pre-seed funding (lead reported as Hi Ventures) for robots that operate the existing machines in high-mix factories. Its "Apprentice Mode" learns a workflow from an operator's demonstration instead of costly integrator programming. Investors and the exact date (early–mid November) vary across sources. ([Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/170164-strive-robotics-raises-3m-pre-seed-to-automate-high-mix-factories/))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- Sonair's ADAR is a 3D ultrasonic sensor for robots that is now safety-certified for human-robot collaboration and shipping to manufacturers in Asia, Europe and North America. One unit covers a 180°×180° field of view out to 5 m, and the company claims it costs 50–80% less than lidar safety scanners. *Opportunity: cheap, certified 3D safety sensing removes one of the most expensive parts of a mobile robot's bill of materials. Lucrative uses include low-cost AMRs and forklifts in warehouses, autonomous floor-cleaning robots, cobots working next to people without fences, and protecting the blind spots of humanoids.* ([Drives & Controls](https://drivesncontrols.com/worlds-first-3d-ultrasonic-safety-sensor-is-cheaper-than-lidar/))
