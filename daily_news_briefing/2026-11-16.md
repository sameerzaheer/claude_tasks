# Daily News Briefing — 2026-11-16

## 🤖 AI / LLMs / AI Products

- Google DeepMind unveiled AlphaProof Code (Nov 10), a multi-agent research system that wrote about 6 million lines of Lean proofs in roughly four weeks, formally verifying key security guarantees of Signal's PQXDH handshake and Triple Ratchet. It caught two deliberately re-introduced past bugs and found no new ones. The artifacts are open-sourced, and there is no commercial API. ([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-deepminds-alphaproof-code-formally-verifies-key-signa-a2c50019))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Wabtec Digital Mine is reportedly partnering with Razor Labs to deploy the DataMind AI predictive-maintenance platform across Wabtec-equipped haul fleets, flagging component failures early enough to plan repairs (announced at IMARC; single secondary report). ([Geomechanics.io](https://www.geomechanics.io/news/article/wabtecrazor-labs-predictive-maintenance-integration-insights-for-mine-fleets))

## 🔍 AI in Mineral Exploration

- A Nov 8 feature says model-guided targeting at Kobra's Ontario projects found new lithium-bearing pegmatites, with surface samples up to 2.08% Li₂O. These are surface samples, not drilled reserves, and the AI attribution comes from a secondary source only. ([Intelligent Living](https://www.intelligentliving.co/ai-mineral-exploration-lithium/))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- At electronica, TDK and Microchip launched a robot-joint sensor-network architecture that pairs InvenSense IMUs with single-pair Ethernet and digital signal controllers, so data is processed locally at each joint. *Opportunity: one thin Ethernet wire per joint, with on-joint processing, cuts wiring cost and weight for many-axis robots. Lucrative uses include humanoid and quadruped limbs, cobot arms with built-in collision and vibration sensing, and retrofit condition-monitoring kits for industrial robot fleets.* ([Electronics USA](https://www.electronics-usa.com/news/117213-tdk-and-microchip-technology-partner-to-enable-sensor-networks-for-robotics))
