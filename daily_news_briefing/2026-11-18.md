## 🤖 AI / LLMs / AI Products

- OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (professional work) and GPT-6.2 Sol (everyday work) on Nov 17 in ChatGPT, Codex and the API. ## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Sandvik showed "Sami", a cabless, battery-electric autonomous surface drill concept run by "Sandi", an AI agent that operators talk to in plain language. Sandi assigns drilling tasks, coordinates the drill with the rest of the fleet and calls in humans only when judgment is needed. This is from Sandvik's Future of Mining event in September; there was no fresher major AI-in-mining news this week. ([Canadian Mining Journal](https://www.canadianminingjournal.com/news/sandvik-reveals-new-autonomous-electric-concept-drill/))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- Grenoble-based MountAIn raised a €7.3M seed round (Nov 17) for software that runs advanced AI, including cloud-grade computer vision, on microcontrollers that cost less than $10, such as Alif Semiconductor's Ensemble and Balletto chips. *Opportunity: vision AI that used to need a GPU or the cloud can now run on battery-powered parts that cost a few dollars. Lucrative uses include always-on smart cameras for retail and security, wireless monitors for wear and conveyor faults in mines and factories, crop and livestock sensors, and low-cost perception for consumer robots and drones.* ([GlobeNewswire](https://globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/17/3404553/0/en/mountain-secures-7-3-million-in-seed-funding-to-run-advanced-ai-on-chips-costing-less-than-10.html))
