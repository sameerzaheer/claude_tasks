# Daily News Briefing — November 26, 2026

## 🤖 AI & LLMs

- OpenAI said on Nov 24 that its GPT-6 models can design proteins by reasoning and writing code, without specialized protein models. In a preliminary test on 30 motif-scaffolding challenges, 43.7% of its designs met the structural criteria versus 27.8% for RFdiffusion, and in wet-lab tests with partner Onava, GPT-6.2-designed minibinders bound all five targets; OpenAI calls the work an early proof of concept. ([Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/openai-reports-gpt-6-designs-proteins-with-reasoning-and-code/))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- At bauma CHINA (Nov 24), SANY unveiled the SKT165Ei, a 100-tonne battery-electric mining truck that drives itself and has no cab, as part of its push toward AI-run mining equipment. This follows SANY's 60 driverless electric trucks, running on Eacon software, at Tibet's Zhibula copper mine. ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/sany-advances-toward-an-ai-native-robotics-future-at-bauma-china-2026-302949365.html))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

- San Francisco's Lupa Robotics raised a $1.67M seed round, reported Nov 25 with Long Journey Ventures as lead (not independently confirmed). It is building a dataset of human hand skill, recorded with sensor gloves and head-mounted cameras, to train robot hands. ([We San Francisco](https://wesanfrancisco.com/news/2026/11/25/lupa-robotics-seed-funding-robot-hand-data))

## 🔧 New Hardware

- Amazon's Ring launched Ring Robotics with two products. The $249.99 Drone Cam is an indoor drone that flies itself; it is due to ship Jan 27, 2027, pending FCC approval. The $999.99 Rover Cam is a patrol robot for businesses that covers up to 5,000 sq ft; it ships Dec 16. ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/11/10/rings-ai-powered-drone-automatically-patrols-inside-your-home/))
  - **Why it's an opportunity:** Indoor robots that fly or drive themselves, with docking and set patrol routes, now cost about as much as consumer gadgets rather than $10k+ industrial systems. That makes cheap indoor monitoring viable for small businesses. Lucrative uses include after-hours patrols of retail and warehouse floors sold as a monthly service, drone counts of stock on high shelves, inspection rounds in server rooms, plant rooms and cold storage, and checks of vacant or short-term rental properties for insurers and property managers.
