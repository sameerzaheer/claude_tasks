## 🔎 AI in Mineral Exploration

- Ivanhoe Mines is paying for GeoIntelX's AI platform to digitize and georeference more than a century of maps and reports held by Angola's geological institute (IGEO), to speed up targeting across its 22,000 km² copper exploration ground in Moxico and Cuando (announced Nov 26 at the Angola International Mining Conference; this is an efficiency step, not a discovery). ([MINING.COM](https://www.mining.com/ivanhoe-mines-taps-ai-to-unlock-angolas-geological-archives/))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- RoboSense said in its Q3 results (Nov 26) that it sold about 427,800 robotics lidar units in the first nine months of 2026, which other reports put at about 423% growth, and that its Space Camera depth camera for robots is now in the supply chains of several leading AI companies (company-reported, unaudited). ([Manila Times / PR Newswire](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/26/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/robosense-reports-third-quarter-2026-unaudited-financial-results-with-robotics-lidar-sales-volume-ranking-no-1-globally/2478429))
  - **Why it's an opportunity:** Robot lidar is now made at car-industry volumes, so 3D sensing is becoming a cheap, off-the-shelf part. Lucrative uses include wire-free robotic lawn mowers and pool cleaners, low-cost autonomous forklifts and tuggers for small warehouses, and lidar-guided retrofit kits for mine and quarry vehicles.
