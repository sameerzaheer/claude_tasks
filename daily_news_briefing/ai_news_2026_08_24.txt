Daily AI News Briefing — August 24, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp (August 21, 2026), its first experimental multimodal model, adding image and screenshot understanding to its existing text and agentic capabilities while claiming performance approaching Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 on several benchmarks. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/21/deepseek-debuts-multimodal-language-model-competitive-with-opus-4-8/)

Google Cloud formally donated its Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol to the Linux Foundation-directed Agentic AI Foundation (August 20, 2026), placing it alongside Anthropic's Model Context Protocol under neutral governance as the foundation's membership grew past 250 organizations including AWS, Microsoft and OpenAI. (https://developers.googleblog.com/en/google-cloud-donates-a2a-to-linux-foundation/)

🚀 Startups

Colossal Biosciences spinout Astromech raised $20 million at a $3.8 billion valuation (announced August 20, 2026), led by biotech investor Bob Nelsen, to build predictive AI models of biological and evolutionary change trained on genomic data spanning roughly 3.8 billion years of evolution. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/20/astromech-raises-20m-to-build-a-biological-operating-system-that-can-forecast-evolutionary-change/)

Twin1 AI emerged from stealth with a $20 million seed round co-led by Bessemer Venture Partners, Tribeca Venture Partners and Aramco Ventures (announced August 20, 2026), building privacy-first AI "digital twins" of knowledge workers that already automate 30-50% of communications work for clients including Linklaters and Orrick. (https://techstartups.com/2026/08/20/twin1-ai-emerges-from-stealth-with-20m-in-funding-to-give-every-professional-an-ai-powered-digital-twin/)

(https://www.constructiondive.com/news/construction-robotics-adoption-investment-nvidia-gravis-bedrock-field-ai/828294/)

Homebuilding startup Rehza launched a connected AI platform for builders and homeowners (announced August 20, 2026), unifying the buyer journey from first interaction through construction, closing and ongoing homeownership into a single digital experience. (https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/20/3348163/0/en/rehza-launches-ai-platform-transforming-the-home-builder-and-homeowner-experience.html)
