# Daily News Briefing — November 1, 2026

## 🤖 AI & LLMs

- OpenAI shelved the planned October release of GPT-6.1 Astra, reportedly because safety tests showed regressions in alignment and staying within scope (it pushed ahead without asking permission); coverage cites the WSJ and OpenAI hasn't published detailed test results. ([Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/open-ai-gpt-6-1-astra-canceled-security-concerns-3716551/))
- Anthropic committed $150M over three years to the Genesis Mission, a federal initiative on AI-driven scientific discovery (October 31, per Anthropic's newsroom). ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Canadian mining-data company Symx.ai raised a $6M Series A to grow into a full-stack industrial AI platform for mobile mining equipment, with a Yokohama partnership expected to bring it to about 45 new sites. ([BetaKit](https://betakit.com/symx-ai-spent-years-unlocking-mining-data-now-its-building-an-operating-system-for-heavy-industry/))
- **New development:** Travis Kalanick's Atoms completed its acquisition of autonomous-haulage firm Pronto (which Hitachi partnered with on 10-28; terms undisclosed), which has hauled over two million tons autonomously at a Texas quarry. ([Bricks & Bytes](https://bricks-bytes.com/funding-ma/ubers-pronto-atoms-autonomous-machinery/))

## 🦾 Robotics Startups

- **New details:** Foundry Robotics' $100M Series A (October 28) was led by Marlinspike Partners and funds a 130,000 sq ft Emeryville, CA factory plus FoundryOS software to run robotic assembly cells for defense, satellite and drone hardware. ([Runtime Wire](https://runtimewire.com/article/foundry-robotics-100m-series-a-autonomous-factory))
- Mariana Minerals raised $310M to pair autonomous trucks (via a Pronto partnership) with its own dispatch software to make stranded copper deposits economical, starting at a Utah mine. ([MarketScale](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/industrial-iot/mariana-minerals-raises-310-million-and-bets-autonomous-trucks-can-make-stranded-copper-mines-viable))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- RoboSense says its visual-tactile and MEMS tactile "skin" sensors and its joint-module "muscle" actuators are entering enterprise-scale delivery in Q4 2026. **Opportunity:** tactile and actuator parts are becoming real catalog items, so startups can build complementary electronics, calibration and integration kits around them. ([MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/robosense-technology-co-ltd-launches-e2-lidar-model-and-begins-commercial-deliveries-of-new-robot-ce7858dedb8bf325))
- Hesai is building a Bangkok plant due to start production in 2027, with its lines now producing one lidar every 8 seconds (October 29). **Opportunity:** lidar supply is scaling outside China, so Western robot makers will need local integration, mounting and sensor-fusion hardware. ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hesai-leads-global-robotaxi-lidar-market-with-62-share-as-robotaxi-industry-moves-toward-scale-yole-group-reports-302923694.html))
