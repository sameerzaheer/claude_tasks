Daily AI News Briefing — August 17, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

OpenAI's unreleased Astra model generated solutions to 10 long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science — each unsolved for a decade or more, including a new construction of non-sofic groups and tighter sphere-packing density bounds — for under $2,000 in inference cost, with all proofs formally verified in Lean 4 (published August 2, 2026). (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/02/openais-astra-solves-10-long-open-math-problems-publishes-proofs/)

OpenAI opened a limited preview of "Ultrafast" mode for GPT-5.6 Sol (announced August 13, 2026), powered by Cerebras hardware, delivering up to 750 output tokens per second — as much as 14x standard throughput — at the same intelligence level, aimed at real-time uses like voice support and live coding. (https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast/)

🚀 Startups

Stripe finalized a deal to acquire AI model-routing platform OpenRouter for more than $7 billion (announced August 16, 2026), a roughly 5x markup from the $1.3 billion valuation OpenRouter fetched in its Series B just three months earlier. (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)

AI video-generation startup Higgsfield raised a $400 million Series B at a $5.4 billion valuation (announced August 17, 2026), led by DST Global with Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Intel Capital and others participating, more than quadrupling its $1.3 billion valuation from January. (https://techfundingnews.com/higgsfield-raises-400m-from-goldman-sachs-dst-global-at-5-4b-valuation/)

🏗️ AI in AEC, Construction & Engineering

Construction-intelligence platform Buildots added survey-grade laser scanning through a partnership with reality-capture specialist NavVis (reported August 11, 2026), unifying precision 3D scans with its existing progress-tracking and BIM data on large projects including Intel semiconductor fab builds. (https://aecmag.com/news/buildots-adds-laser-scanning-through-navvis-partnership)

MEP-engineering automation startup Endra expanded into the US and UK after a $50 million raise (reported early August 2026), pitching AI that ingests engineering firms' existing files to automate system design, routing and documentation as data-center, electrification and net-zero projects outstrip the supply of qualified engineers. (https://hackernoon.com/endra-expands-to-the-us-with-$50m-firepower-as-data-center-boom-outruns-engineering-workforce)

📋 AI in Procurement & Proposal Writing

(https://www.gep.com/blog/technology/ai-ready-procurement-team-capabilities)
