## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- New development on Hesai (previously covered Oct 29): its Q3 results (Nov 10) show lidar shipments up about 65% year over year to 729,673 units, with its robotic actuation modules now powering Sharpa's new tactile-sensing robot and dexterous hand. *Opportunity: actuators and tactile sensing are becoming bundled supplier categories, leaving room for specialized fingertip sensors and compact joint hardware.* ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399191/0/en/hesai-group-reports-third-quarter-2026-unaudited-financial-results.html))
- Camsense, a lesser-known Chinese lidar maker supplying Roborock and Xiaomi robot vacuums, has nearly doubled its sensor shipments since 2023 (Nov 9, trade report). *Opportunity: low-cost consumer-grade lidar is now cheap enough to retrofit onto small industrial and service robots.* ([Vacuum Wars](https://vacuumwars.com/camsense-robot-vacuum-lidar/))
