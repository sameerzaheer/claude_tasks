## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- LinkerBot's Linker Hand O6, a dexterous robot hand, sells for 6,666 yuan (about $912), with a subsidized O6 Lite at 3,999 yuan, as the company seeks a reported $6B valuation to mass-produce cheap hands. *Opportunity: a dexterous hand at under $1,000 (they used to cost $5,000–$15,000) makes it affordable to put hands on cobots and low-cost arms. Lucrative uses include picking mixed items in e-commerce warehouses, sorting returns, tending lab equipment, assembling small electronics, and sorting core samples or ore in mine labs.* ([KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com/linkerbot-raises-new-funding-to-build-a-full-stack-platform-for-robot-dexterity))
