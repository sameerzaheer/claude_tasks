## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- **New development on RoboSense:** its Q3 2026 results show record lidar sales, with 427,800 of 1,314,800 units sold in the first nine months going to robotics, and volume deliveries of its actuator-type "muscles" line slated for Q4. **Opportunity:** robot builders now buy sensing and actuation as bundled component sets, so niche suppliers can win by offering cheaper or specialized parts that drop into those stacks. ([Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/market-industry/2108495209121140737))
- Yole Group ranked Hesai the top robotaxi lidar supplier with 62% share (October 29; company-announced, so self-reported). **Opportunity:** lidar is consolidating around a few Chinese vendors, leaving room for non-Chinese or application-specific sensors where buyers want supply diversity. ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/hesai-leads-global-robotaxi-lidar-market-with-62-share-as-robotaxi-industry-moves-toward-scale-yole-group-reports-302923694.html))
