# Daily News Briefing — 2026-11-21

> Note: little fresh AI-in-exploration news turned up this week that hadn't already been covered, so that item is a smaller business update.

## 🤖 AI / LLMs / AI Products

- Google released Gemini 4 Neon (Nov 18), a mid-tier model between Hydrogen and Carbon, which Google says runs about 3x faster than Carbon at about 60% lower cost ($3/$15 per million input/output tokens); it is rolling out in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity and the Gemini app (speed and cost are Google's own figures). ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon/))

## ⛏️ AI in Mining

- Epiroc and Luck Stone will remotely run an autonomy-equipped SmartROC D65 surface drill at a Virginia quarry live from the CES 2027 floor in Las Vegas (Jan 6–9), more than 2,000 miles away, to show off Epiroc's automation that works across equipment brands (announced Nov 20). ([Canadian Mining Journal](https://canadianminingjournal.com/news/luck-stone-epiroc-to-showcase-remote-controlled-drilling-live-at-ces))

([e27](https://e27.co/singapores-botsync-takes-its-physical-ai-pitch-to-the-us-with-us11m-in-fresh-funding-20261118/))

## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Opportunities

- Foundation unveiled its Phantom MKII humanoid (Nov 19), which it says carries 200 lb through rain, snow and dust, uses redesigned actuators and costs half as much to build as the MK1; production is targeted for early 2027. *Opportunity: a weatherproof humanoid that carries heavy loads at half the build cost can work outdoors and in dirty places that indoor humanoids can't. Lucrative uses include carrying materials on construction sites, loading and unloading trucks in yards, carrying tools and supplies in mines and tunnels, and moving heavy loads in shipyards and steel mills.* ([Fast Company](https://fastcompany.com/91643258/foundation-future-industries-humanoid-robot-war))
