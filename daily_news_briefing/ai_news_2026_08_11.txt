Daily AI News Briefing — August 11, 2026

🤖 AI & LLMs

Anthropic, Macquarie Asset Management and GIC announced "Theseus Infrastructure" (August 10, 2026), a new venture that will develop, own and lease dedicated AI data centers to Anthropic at scale, with Anthropic pledging to cover 100% of grid-upgrade costs and any consumer electricity price increases tied to the buildout. (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/anthropic-macquarie-and-gic-form-venture-for-ai-data-centers)

Meta released Muse Glimmer (August 10, 2026), a 30-billion-parameter open-weight (Apache 2.0) agentic model tuned for local coding, tool use and LLM-as-judge tasks that runs on a single consumer GPU once 4-bit quantized to under 20GB. (https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/10/meta-releases-open-source-muse-glimmer-model-30b-parameters/)

(https://fortune.com/2026/08/10/exclusive-corma-raises-60-million-from-sequoia-for-ai-trained-to-defend-against-cyberattacks/)

(https://fortune.com/2026/07/22/arrakis-a-startup-betting-ais-biggest-payoff-is-in-industrial-sectors-not-office-work-emerges-from-stealth-with-38-million-in-venture-funding/)
