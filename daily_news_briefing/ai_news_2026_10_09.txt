🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

TrendForce says MEMS sensing is shifting from single-mode to multimodal for humanoid edge AI and e-skin, with a single dexterous hand carrying an estimated 80-200 pressure sensors plus IMU and ToF — opportunity: validation happens in 2026-27, so sensor makers who win design-ins now are positioned for volume in 2028-30. (https://www.trendforce.com/research/download/RP260910XF)

Analysis of humanoid funding shows tactile-sensing startups raising a few million dollars against single $1B+ humanoid rounds (though PaXini raised $145M in March) — opportunity: touch sensing is an underfunded bottleneck, so low-cost, manufacturable tactile skins and force/torque sensors have room.
