## 🔧 Hardware / Sensor Automation Opportunities

- Boston Dynamics unveiled a four-fingered Atlas hand with 13 actuated degrees of freedom and tactile sensing in the fingertips and palm (October 1). **Opportunity:** humanoid hands are becoming a sensor-dense component, creating demand for third-party tactile sensors, miniature actuators and fingertip electronics. ([RoboticsReports](https://roboticsreports.com/what-is-new-with-research-robotics-in-october-2026-latest-company-releases-and-research-papers-and-key-takeaways/))
- DH-Robotics debuted the ADH-5-13, a five-finger direct-drive hand with 13 active DoF and fingertip/palm pressure sensors, shown at IROS 2026. **Opportunity:** off-the-shelf dexterous hands let robot builders skip hand development, shifting value to tactile sensing, grip software and application-specific end-effectors. ([RoboticsReports](https://roboticsreports.com/what-is-new-with-research-robotics-in-october-2026-latest-company-releases-and-research-papers-and-key-takeaways/))
